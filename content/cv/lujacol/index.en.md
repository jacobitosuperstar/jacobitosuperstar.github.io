### Company Internal Tools

Personal company, founded and run alongside my employed roles, and later sold.
I built the software the operation ran on.

- The inventory management system, and a delivery calendar that synced
dynamically with the Hapag-Lloyd carrier, tracking the vessel that was
carrying each order.

- A web portal for my clients to create orders, keep track of the shipping
status and download required documents for merchandise nationalization.

- Automatic emails for reminders, ISO documentation expiry alerts and
important notes about the merchandise, which helped me keep the head count
short on the company, prepare the container in less than a week, and to work
as lean as possible, minimizing expenses.
